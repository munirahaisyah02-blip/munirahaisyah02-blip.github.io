Broiler Growth & Derivative Analysis

In Malaysian poultry production, we model the growth of broiler chickens (e.g., Ross 308) using a Logistic Growth Function. For an Agro Product Tech student, the 1st derivative is vital because it identifies the Point of Inflection—the age where growth rate peaks before slowing down.

Growth Function: f(t) = K / (1 + e-r(t - t₀))
Derivative (Rate): f'(t) = r · f(t) · (1 - f(t)/K)
