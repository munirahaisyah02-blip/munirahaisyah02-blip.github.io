FFB Oil Synthesis Dynamics

As an Agro Product Tech student, you know that mesocarp oil accumulation is non-linear. We use the derivative of the oil percentage function to identify the Peak Accumulation Phase.

Oil Content f(t) = L / (1 + e-k(t - t₀))
Synthesis Rate f'(t) = (k · L · e-k(t - t₀)) / (1 + e-k(t - t₀))²
